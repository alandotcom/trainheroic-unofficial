For help with TrainHeroic Unofficial, email
[support@trainheroic-unofficial.com](mailto:support@trainheroic-unofficial.com).

Include what you were trying to do, which surface you were using (hosted connector, local MCP,
CLI, SDK, or browser export), what happened, and what you expected. Error text is useful, but do
not send your TrainHeroic password, session token, OAuth token, or unnecessary athlete data.

This inbox supports the unofficial integration only. For the TrainHeroic product, billing, or
your TrainHeroic account, contact TrainHeroic directly.

You can also [report a software issue on GitHub](https://github.com/alandotcom/trainheroic-unofficial/issues)
if it does not contain private account or athlete information.
