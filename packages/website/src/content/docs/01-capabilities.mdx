The connector changes its tool set to match the account you sign in with. A coach account also
has athlete scope, so coaches can query their own training alongside their roster. Choose a
role to see what Claude can do for it.

<View title="Coach" icon="clipboard-list">

## Your roster

- Browse athletes, teams, programs, notifications, and recent roster activity.
- Pull readiness, compliance, volume, lift history, and main-lift PR reports.
- Invite, archive, and restore athletes, and manage teams and join codes.

<Prompt description="Find athletes who need a check-in." actions={["copy"]}>
  Which athletes on my roster have not trained in the last seven days?
</Prompt>

## Your programming

- Build workouts as drafts, read them back, publish them, copy them, or save them as templates.
- Resolve exercise names against the TrainHeroic exercise library before anything is written.
- Prescribe or log sets for one athlete, or swap an exercise for that athlete only.

<Prompt description="Draft a session for review." actions={["copy"]}>
  Draft a five-by-five back squat session for Monday on my strength team, then read it back to
  me before publishing.
</Prompt>

## Your messages

- Read conversations and draft messages to athletes or teams.
- Send or delete a message after you approve it.

## Your training

- Review scheduled and completed workouts across a date range.
- See what is on today's log and add notes or RPE to a workout or exercise.
- Create a session for unprogrammed training and log it set by set.

<Prompt description="Plan around today's session." actions={["copy"]}>
  What is on my program today, and what did I lift for those exercises last time?
</Prompt>

## Your progress

- Search the exercises you have logged and inspect the full history for one lift.
- Read personal records, working maxes, benchmark leaderboards, and lifetime totals.
- Export your workout history as CSV, JSON, or plain text, from Claude or from the
  [browser export](/export).

<Prompt description="Review a lift's trend." actions={["copy"]}>
  Compare my last five back squat sessions and summarize the trend.
</Prompt>

## Confirmation is part of the tool

Publishing, sending, deleting, archiving, and logging athlete-facing results are gated inside
the MCP server. A client that supports MCP elicitation shows a confirmation prompt. Other
clients must pass an explicit confirmation value, and the operation fails closed without it.
The [tool catalog](/developers/mcp/tools) marks every tool that confirms.

:::info
The model can draft a workout or message without confirmation. Confirmation is required when
the action becomes visible to an athlete or changes remote data.
:::

## Undocumented API

This project drives the same private endpoints used by the TrainHeroic web app. Those endpoints
can change without notice, so treat the integration as a practical toolkit rather than an
official compatibility guarantee. The [terms](/terms) describe what that means for your use.
